Funky Launcher — help

How to install Cyberpunk 2077 mods

The easiest way to install lots of mods for Cyberpunk 2077 is a ready modlist and the free Funky Launcher: it checks your game, downloads the mods and builds them in a separate folder. Your game files are barely touched.

What you need

  • Cyberpunk 2077 version 2.5 with the Phantom Liberty expansion and the free REDmod add-on. Without REDmod the modlist may not start; the launcher checks for it.
  • A Nexus Mods account. A free one is enough.
  • About 40 GB of free disk space for the modlist, plus the game itself.
  • Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit).

How to install the Neon Nights modlist

  • Download the launcher from https://funkylauncher.com/en and start it with a normal double-click, not "Run as administrator".
  • Pick the Neon Nights modlist.
  • The launcher finds the game by itself. If it doesn't, press "Show the game folder" and choose the folder with the game. Choose a separate empty folder for the modlist, not the game folder.
  • Press INSTALL. The launcher checks the game version and files and shows the "Nexus login — 3 steps" window: Wabbajack opens, press Settings, then Log in on the Nexus Mods line and confirm access in your browser. The launcher notices the login by itself.
  • Wait for the download. With Nexus Premium files download by themselves. Without Premium you press "Slow download" once for each mod and the launcher walks you through it; it takes an hour or two. You can close the window and continue later from the same place.
  • When the launcher says it's done, press PLAY. Start the game only with this button, not with the game's own shortcut: otherwise the mods won't load.

What the launcher does to your game

The mods live in their own folder. The launcher puts only 6 small loader files (CET, RED4ext, redscript) into the game folder: the mods need them to start. The "Restore clean game" button removes them.

Good to know

  • The modlist is made for game version 2.5. Turn off the game's auto-update and don't update it until a new modlist version appears in the launcher.
  • We don't re-upload other authors' mods: all of them are downloaded from their authors' pages on Nexus Mods (except our own fixed build of ArchiveXL under the MIT license). Please endorse the authors.

Do I need Nexus Premium?

No. A free Nexus account is enough: without Premium you press "Slow download" once for each mod, which takes roughly an hour or two. With Premium everything downloads by itself.

How much disk space does it need?

About 40 GB for the Neon Nights modlist with the downloaded files, plus the game itself.

Can I get back a clean game?

Yes. The "Restore clean game" button removes the loader files from the game folder. The mods and the modlist stay in their own folder.
