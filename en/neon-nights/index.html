What is Neon Nights

Neon Nights is a ready-made modlist for Cyberpunk 2077 with Phantom Liberty: hundreds of mods (the number depends on the version), already picked and set up to work together. It changes what you see every minute: light and weather, the streets of Night City, apartments, clothes, cars and combat. It adds new things to do and the small details that make the city feel alive: your summoned car pulls up right next to you and follows you, there is an autopilot, dual wielding and a new third-person view. The story of the game stays where it is.

Who it is for

For players who want to come back to Night City and find it different, without spending evenings hunting for mods, checking requirements and untangling conflicts. You don't need to know what RED4ext or ArchiveXL is: Funky Launcher checks your game and PC, downloads the mods from their authors' pages on Nexus Mods and builds everything in a separate folder. If you like fine-tuning, you can switch individual mods off right in the launcher; the ones the modlist depends on stay locked.

Standard, High or Ultra

Standard is the base of the modlist: pick it if your PC is close to the lower end of the guideline. High is Standard plus graphics and background mods. Ultra adds heavier graphics on top and is meant for a powerful graphics card. The launcher looks at your PC and suggests a version. A guideline for the modlist as a whole (not measured per version): Core i7-12700 or Ryzen 7 7800X3D, RTX 2060 Super or RX 5700 XT with 8 GB of video memory, 16 GB of RAM, SSD: about 70 GB for the game and about 40 GB for the modlist. For 1440p and 4K you need a stronger graphics card.

FAQ

What do I need to install Neon Nights?

Cyberpunk 2077 version 2.5 with Phantom Liberty and the free REDmod add-on, a Nexus Mods account (a free one works), Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and free disk space. The launcher checks this before installing.

Do I need Nexus Premium?

No. With Premium the mods download on their own; without it the launcher guides you step by step, and you press "Slow download" once on each mod page.

What happens to my game?

The modlist is installed into a separate folder. The launcher puts only a few small loader files into the game folder, because the mods need them to run; the "Restore clean game" button removes them.

What if the game crashes?

The launcher has Verify files, Repair, a return to the previous modlist version and an error report. After a crash a window shows which mod may be the cause.

Can I turn off a mod I don't want?

Yes, in the "Modlist mods" window. Keep in mind that switching a mod off in the middle of a playthrough can break your saves. By default the launcher backs up your saves before every game launch from the launcher.

How it installs

The launcher checks your game, downloads the mods from Nexus and builds the modlist in its own folder. With a Nexus Premium account everything downloads by itself; without Premium the launcher walks you through it: for each mod you press "Slow download" once — it takes longer, but the modlist is the same. The launcher puts only 6 small loader files (CET, RED4ext, redscript) into the game folder, because the mods need them to run. "Restore clean game" removes them. Start the game with the "Play" button in the launcher.
