Neon Nights

a modlist for Cyberpunk 2077

Neon Nights is a Cyberpunk 2077 modlist I built and tested myself. The goal: a more beautiful, alive and interesting Night City without turning it into a different game. The mods are picked and configured to work together.

Download Funky Launcher

The launcher is free. Pick the modlist in the launcher's library.

In short

  • Game: Cyberpunk 2077 version 2.5 + Phantom Liberty.
  • Mods: 400+ in the base (more with the Russian translations).
  • Modlist version: Russian or English, chosen in the launcher.
  • You need: the installed game in the right version and a Nexus Mods account (a free one works).

What changes in the game

🌆 Visuals, lighting & weather. HD textures, new lighting for Night City and Dogtown, weather, rain and water, switchable LUTs, distant shadows.

🏙 Living city & gameplay. Street events, gangs on the streets and smarter police, taxi work, roulette and blackjack, new quests and contracts, merc allies, skill trainers, ragdoll physics, melee. Loot markers on containers (see what's inside), auto-loot, a flashlight, stat reset at any time, launch without intro videos, skins and weapon names in crafting.

👗 Shops & clothing. Virtual shops on V's computer: hundreds of items, shoes, accessories, jewelry.

💇 Hair. 50+ new hairstyles, many with physics.

🧑 Body & face. Detailed faces and skin, eyes, makeup, tattoos, first-person body, Appearance Menu Mod.

❤️ Romance & NPCs. Judy, Panam, River and Kerry: dates and new conversations.

🍸 Characters. Talk to fixers, bartenders, vendors and ripperdocs. V talks more and has new lines (in the Russian version — with Russian voice-over for these lines).

🏠 Apartments. Upgraded vanilla ones and new homes: villa, mansion, penthouse with garage.

🚗 Cars. Real supercars and classics, car dealer, cockpit view, vehicle summoning.

📸 Photo mode, 📻 radio, 🖥 customizable HUD and handy menus.

🔧 All the frameworks needed (CET, RED4ext, redscript, ArchiveXL, TweakXL, Codeware), fixes and crash protection.

How it installs

The launcher checks your game, downloads the mods from Nexus and builds the modlist in its own folder. With a Nexus Premium account everything downloads by itself; without Premium the launcher walks you through it: for each mod you press "Slow download" once — it takes longer, but the modlist is the same. The launcher puts only 6 small loader files (CET, RED4ext, redscript) into the game folder, because the mods need them to run. "Restore clean game" removes them. Start the game with the "Play" button in the launcher.

What you need

  • Cyberpunk 2077 version 2.5 with Phantom Liberty.
  • A Nexus Mods account (a free one works).
  • Disk space: about 40 GB for the modlist with the downloaded files, plus the game itself.
  • Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). The modlist was built and checked on a PC with an i7-13700HX, RTX 4080 Laptop and 32 GB RAM; minimum requirements have not been checked yet.

Not included

The DreamVoice Russian voice-over for Phantom Liberty — its authors do not allow it in modlists; install it separately from the authors' page (the link is on the modlist card in the launcher).
