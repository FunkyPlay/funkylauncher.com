Funky Launcher and modlists: frequently asked questions

Short answers to the questions people ask most about Funky Launcher and the modlists for Cyberpunk 2077, Starfield and Fallout 4.

In short

  • The launcher and the modlists are currently free.
  • You need the right game version, a free Nexus Mods account and Windows 10 or 11.
  • The mods live in a separate folder, and the "Restore clean game" button removes the loader files from the game folder.

Is it free?

Yes, the launcher and the modlists are currently free. You need to own the game yourself: we neither sell nor share it.

Do I need Nexus Premium?

No. A free Nexus Mods account is enough: without Premium you press "Slow download" once for each mod and the launcher walks you through it; it takes roughly an hour or two. With Premium the files download by themselves.

Do I need a Nexus Mods account?

Yes. Mods are downloaded from their authors' pages through your account. You log in once: the launcher shows the "Nexus login — 3 steps" window and notices the login by itself.

Which games and versions are supported?

Three modlists: Neon Nights for Cyberpunk 2077 (patch 2.5 with Phantom Liberty, the free REDmod add-on is required), Nova Nights for Starfield (version 1.16.244 with Shattered Space) and Atomic Nights for Fallout 4 (version 1.11.240 and all 6 expansions). Nova Nights and Atomic Nights are still in testing.

How much disk space do I need?

About 40 GB for Neon Nights, about 50 GB for Nova Nights (HD planets are optional and take about 21 GB more) and about 90 GB for Atomic Nights. This includes the downloaded mod archives; the game itself is extra.

Is it safe?

We don't re-upload other authors' mods (except our own fixed build of ArchiveXL under the MIT license): they are downloaded from their authors' pages on Nexus Mods, and the launcher puts only small loader files into the game folder, which the "Restore clean game" button removes. Download the launcher only from our website. The launcher doesn't have a digital signature yet, so Windows may show a warning: press "More info" and "Run anyway". Antivirus software may raise a false alarm on mod loaders; if you are not sure, check the file on VirusTotal. Add antivirus exclusions only if you downloaded the launcher from our website; don't turn the antivirus off completely.

Will it break my game?

The modlists don't replace the game: the mods live in a separate folder, and the "Restore clean game" button removes the loader files from the game folder. It is better not to update the game and to turn off its auto-update: the modlists are made for a specific version.

Why shouldn't I update the game?

Mods and loaders are tied to a specific game version. After a game update they often stop working until a new modlist version is out. A new modlist version arrives in the launcher by itself.

The game crashes. What do I do?

Check the game version, start the game with the PLAY button and press "VERIFY FILES" in "Install and verify". If that doesn't help, press "Make an error report" on Home and send it to the support chat in the launcher. We go through reports every day.

What kind of computer do I need?

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). We haven't measured minimum requirements for the modlists yet; use the game's own requirements as a guide. The modlists were checked on a laptop with an i7-13700HX, an RTX 4080 Laptop and 32 GB of RAM. If your graphics card has 8 GB of memory or less, the launcher will recommend lightweight graphics.

Can I get back a clean game?

Yes. Open the Library and press "Restore clean game" on the installed modlist. More in the article "How to remove mods and restore a clean game".

How do I contact support?

The launcher has a support chat: the "Contact support" button at the bottom left. If something doesn't work, first press "Make an error report" on Home and send the archive to the chat.

Can I add my own mods?

You shouldn't: the modlist is tuned as a whole, and added mods or a changed load order may break it. Don't update mods inside the modlist.
