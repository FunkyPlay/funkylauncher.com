Cyberpunk 2077 crashing with mods: what to do

If Cyberpunk 2077 crashes or won't start after you install mods, the cause is most often one of a few simple things. Go through them in order — it is usually enough.

Check in this order

  • Game version. Mods are made for a specific version; for the Neon Nights modlist it is 2.5 with Phantom Liberty. If the game updated, mods often stop working. Turn off the game's auto-update and don't update it until a new modlist version is out.
  • REDmod. You need the free REDmod add-on: after installing, the file tools\redmod\bin\redMod.exe must exist in the game folder. Without it the mods won't load and the game may not start.
  • How you start. Start the game only with the PLAY button in Funky Launcher. If you start it with a shortcut, the mods won't load, and the loader files left in the game folder can break the launch.
  • Game files. In the launcher open "Install and verify" → "VERIFY FILES". If the game files differ from the needed version, the launcher will say so.
  • Antivirus. It may have deleted or blocked the loader files (version.dll, winmm.dll, RED4ext). If the error window names these files, press "REPAIR" or reinstall the modlist through the launcher.
  • Launcher and driver. Update the launcher to the latest version and update your graphics driver.

If it didn't help

Right after a failed launch, press "Make an error report" on Home: an archive appears on your desktop. Send it to the support chat in the launcher and describe in words what happens: a black screen, an error window (text or screenshot), whether the game closes at once or on the splash screen. This helps find the cause much faster.

How we work on crashes

Every day we go through player reports, update mods and write our own fixes, so the modlist gets more stable with each update. We look into every issue you send and do our best to fix it.

The game crashes right after launch. Where do I start?

Check the game version (2.5 with Phantom Liberty), that REDmod is installed, and that you start the game with the PLAY button in the launcher. Then press "VERIFY FILES" in "Install and verify".

The game crashed after a game update. What now?

Mods are made for a specific version. Wait for a new modlist version: it will appear in the launcher by itself. It is better to turn off the game's auto-update.

How do I send an error report?

On Home press "Make an error report"; the archive is saved on your desktop. Send it to the support chat in the launcher and describe what happens after you press PLAY.
