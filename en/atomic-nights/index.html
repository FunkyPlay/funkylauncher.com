Atomic Nights

a modlist for Fallout 4

Atomic Nights is a "vanilla+" modlist for Fallout 4: the same game, only more stable, beautiful and handy, without turning it into a different one. I built it myself; the mods are picked to work together.

Download Funky Launcher

The launcher is free. Pick the modlist in the launcher's library.

In short

  • Game: Fallout 4 version 1.11.240 + all 6 DLC.
  • Mods: 180+.
  • Modlist version: Russian or English, chosen in the launcher. Some mods stay in English where there is no translation.
  • DLSS 4 / FSR 3.1 — a checkbox, on by default; frame generation is off by default (results depend on your graphics card).
  • Russian voice-over for the whole game and DLC is a third-party add-on by its author: switched on with a checkbox, the file is taken from the author's page on Nexus.
  • You need: the installed game in the right version with all DLC and a Nexus Mods account (a free one works).

What changes in the game

🛠 Base & fixes. F4SE, crash and bug fixes, the mod settings menu (MCM).

🖥 Interface. FallUI: inventory, workbench, map, HUD; full lines in dialogues.

🌆 Visuals. New textures, weather, stars, lighting, a crisp picture.

🧑 Characters. HD faces, hairstyles, eyes, teeth, the LooksMenu appearance editor, full body in first person.

⚙️ Gameplay. Faster reloads, weapons on the body, a Pip-Boy flashlight, companions.

🗺 Quests & locations. New stories, Commonwealth islands and caves, encounters on the road.

🏘 Settlements. Workshop Framework, Homemaker, more building options and settlers.

🧥 Armor & clothing. Upgraded armor, backpacks, concealed armor.

🔫 Weapons. New weapons that fit the game.

🔊 Sound. Realistic reverb, footsteps, power armor and weapon sounds.

How it installs

The launcher checks your game (version 1.11.240 and all DLC), downloads the mods from Nexus and builds the modlist in its own folder. With a Nexus Premium account everything downloads by itself; without Premium the launcher walks you through it: for each mod you press "Slow download" once — it takes longer, but the modlist is the same. The launcher puts the F4SE loader into the game folder itself (2 files), and "Restore clean game" removes it. Start the game with the "Play" button in the launcher.

What you need

  • Fallout 4 version 1.11.240 + all 6 DLC.
  • A Nexus Mods account (a free one works).
  • Disk space: the game + about 90 GB for the modlist and mod archives (Russian voice-over: ~10 GB more).
  • Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). The modlist was run on a PC with an i7-13700HX, RTX 4080 Laptop and 32 GB RAM (the Russian version); the English version has not been launched yet. Minimum requirements have not been checked.

Russian voice-over

The AI voice-over "Russian voice for Fallout 4" 2.4 (https://www.nexusmods.com/fallout4/mods/85110) is a third-party add-on by its author: switched on with a checkbox, the file is taken from the author's page on Nexus. Not checked with version 1.11.240.
