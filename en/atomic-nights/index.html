What is Atomic Nights

Atomic Nights is a modlist for Fallout 4 version 1.11.240 with all six expansions, 170+ mods (the exact number depends on the version). The style is "vanilla plus": the same Fallout 4, only more stable, better looking and handier. Inside are engine and settlement fixes, a comfortable inventory and workbench, new textures, light and weather, detailed faces, weapons and armour in the spirit of the game, new stories and places in the Commonwealth, extended settlement building and more realistic sound.

Who it is for

For players who want to replay Fallout 4 with fixes for old annoyances and common causes of crashes, but without changing the rules of the game. You don't have to install F4SE by hand, hunt for fixes or arrange the load order: Funky Launcher checks the game version and expansions, downloads the mods from their authors' pages on Nexus Mods and builds the modlist in a separate folder. You can switch individual mods off in the launcher, while the ones the modlist depends on stay locked.

Standard, High or Ultra

Standard is the base of the modlist; guideline: Core i7-4790 or Ryzen 5 1500X, GTX 1060 6 GB or RX 580 8 GB. High adds mods that run in the background all the time: more people, more encounters and new enemy behaviour in combat. The same graphics card will do, but these mods load the processor, so 6 cores or more are needed (i5-8400 or Ryzen 5 2600). Ultra adds HD graphics on top (textures, faces, distant views): it also needs 6 cores or more and a graphics card from RTX 2060 or RX 6600 with 8 GB of video memory. All versions need 16 GB of RAM and an SSD: about 85 GB for the modlist (about 90 GB for Ultra) plus the game itself. This is a guideline, not a measurement. The launcher suggests a version for your PC; it is best to change it on a new game.

FAQ

What do I need to install Atomic Nights?

Fallout 4 version 1.11.240 with all 6 expansions, a Nexus Mods account (a free one works), Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and disk space. The launcher checks this before installing.

Do I need Nexus Premium?

No. With Premium the mods download on their own; without it the launcher guides you step by step, and you press "Slow download" once on each mod page.

What happens to my game?

The modlist is installed into a separate folder. The launcher puts only 2 small F4SE loader files into the game folder; the "Restore clean game" button removes them.

What if the game crashes?

The launcher has Verify files, Repair, a return to the previous modlist version and an error report.

Can I turn off a mod I don't want?

Yes, in the "Modlist mods" window. Keep in mind that switching a mod off in the middle of a playthrough can break your saves. By default the launcher backs up your saves before every game launch from the launcher.

How it installs

The launcher checks your game (version 1.11.240 and all DLC), downloads the mods from Nexus and builds the modlist in its own folder. With a Nexus Premium account everything downloads by itself; without Premium the launcher walks you through it: for each mod you press "Slow download" once — it takes longer, but the modlist is the same. The launcher puts the F4SE loader into the game folder itself (2 files), and "Restore clean game" removes it. Start the game with the "Play" button in the launcher.
