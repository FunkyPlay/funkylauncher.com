Nova Nights

a modlist for Starfield

Nova Nights is a Starfield modlist I built myself. The goal: a handier, more beautiful and livelier game without turning it into a different one. The mods are picked to work together.

Download Funky Launcher

The launcher is free. Pick the modlist in the launcher's library.

In short

  • Game: Starfield version 1.16.244 + Shattered Space.
  • Mods: 400+.
  • Modlist version: Russian or English, chosen in the launcher. In the Russian version some mod names stay in English.
  • HD planets are an add-on with a separate checkbox (~21 GB, off by default).
  • You need: the installed game in the right version and a Nexus Mods account (a free one works).

What changes in the game

🛠 Base & fixes. The SFSE script extender, engine fixes, a big unofficial patch, crash protection and an error log.

🖥 Interface. StarUI: inventory, workbench, ship builder, outposts; handy item sorting, a clear ship HUD.

⚔️ Gameplay & combat. Skills, combat and balance without needless difficulty; easier lockpicking and persuasion; vendors with double credits; instant scanning.

🤖 Enemies. Smarter AI: cover and tactics for Spacers, Pirates, Ecliptic, Va'ruun and Starborn; enemies boarding your ship in combat.

📜 Quests & world. New quests, points of interest, procedural content.

🏙 Cities & locations. Livelier cities, new and improved places; more variety among NPCs — citizens per city, Paradiso, Starborn, the First, Freestar militia.

🌌 Visuals, weather & sound. A rich, haze-free picture, weather and lighting, Atmospheric Audio.

👥 Companions & crew. New companions, crew at ship stations.

🧑 Looks. Detailed faces and skin, hairstyles, makeup.

🧥 Clothing & spacesuits. Hundreds of items and spacesuits, unique faction suits (Ecliptic, Va'ruun, Infiltrator, Spacers).

🔫 Weapons. New weapons that fit the game.

🚀 Ships. New ships and parts, ship building without needless limits; seamless grav jumps and takeoffs; no intro videos.

🏠 Outposts & homes. More building options, new homes.

🚙 Vehicles. Ground vehicles.

How it installs

The launcher checks your game, downloads the mods from Nexus and builds the modlist in its own folder. With a Nexus Premium account everything downloads by itself; without Premium the launcher walks you through it: for each mod you press "Slow download" once — it takes longer, but the modlist is the same. The launcher puts the SFSE loader into the game folder itself (2 files), and "Restore clean game" removes it. Start the game with the "Play" button in the launcher.

What you need

  • Starfield version 1.16.244 + Shattered Space.
  • A Nexus Mods account (a free one works).
  • Disk space: the game + about 50 GB for the modlist and mod archives (HD planets: ~21 GB more).
  • Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit).
