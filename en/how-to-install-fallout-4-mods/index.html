Funky Launcher — help

How to install Fallout 4 mods

The easiest way to install lots of mods for Fallout 4 is a ready modlist and the free Funky Launcher: it checks your game, downloads the mods and builds them in a separate folder. The game version must be 1.11.240.

What you need

  • Fallout 4 version 1.11.240 and all 6 expansions: Automatron, Wasteland Workshop, Far Harbor, Contraptions Workshop, Vault-Tec Workshop and Nuka-World. Without them the modlist won't install; the launcher checks for it.
  • A Nexus Mods account. A free one is enough.
  • About 90 GB of free disk space for the modlist and mod archives, plus the game itself. An SSD is better.
  • 8 GB of RAM, 16 GB is better. Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit).

How to install the Atomic Nights modlist

  • Download the launcher from https://funkylauncher.com/en/download and start it with a normal double-click, not "Run as administrator".
  • Pick the Atomic Nights modlist.
  • The launcher finds the game by itself. If it doesn't, press "Show the game folder" and choose the folder with the game. Choose a separate empty folder for the modlist, not the game folder.
  • Press INSTALL. The launcher checks the game version and expansions and shows the "Nexus login — 3 steps" window: Wabbajack opens, press Settings, then Log in on the Nexus Mods line and confirm access in your browser. The launcher notices the login by itself.
  • Wait for the download. With Nexus Premium files download by themselves. Without Premium you press "Slow download" once for each mod and the launcher walks you through it; it takes an hour or two. You can close the window and continue later from the same place.
  • When the launcher says it's done, press PLAY. Start the game only with this button, not with the game's own shortcut: otherwise the mods won't load. The first launch takes longer than usual — wait 2–3 minutes while the plugins load.
  • At the start of a new game a few mod message boxes in English will appear — press Enter to close each one. Loading pauses until you do.

What the launcher does to your game

The mods live in their own folder. The launcher puts only the F4SE loader into the game folder: 2 files. The "Restore clean game" button removes them. The modlist's saves are kept apart from your regular game saves — this is by design.

Good to know

  • The modlist is made for game version 1.11.240. Turn off the game's auto-update and don't update it until a new modlist version appears in the launcher.
  • Don't update mods inside the modlist and don't add your own: it may break it.
  • The Atomic Nights modlist is still in testing. If something doesn't work, press "Make an error report" on Home and send it to the support chat in the launcher.
  • We don't re-upload other authors' mods: all of them are downloaded from their authors' pages on Nexus Mods. The launcher makes two small fixes on your computer after download; we don't re-upload or share the authors' files. Please endorse the authors.

Do I need Nexus Premium?

No. A free Nexus account is enough: without Premium you press "Slow download" once for each mod, which takes roughly an hour or two. With Premium everything downloads by itself.

Which Fallout 4 version do I need?

1.11.240 with all six expansions. The modlist is made for it: if the game updated or an expansion is missing, the launcher will tell you.

How much disk space does it need?

About 90 GB for the Atomic Nights modlist with the downloaded files, plus the game itself.

Can I get back a clean game?

Yes. The "Restore clean game" button removes the F4SE files from the game folder. The mods and the modlist stay in their own folder.
